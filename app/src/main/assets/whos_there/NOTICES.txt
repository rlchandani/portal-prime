   Source: https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/face_detector/blaze_face_short_range/float16/latest/
   Copyright Google LLC. Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0.
   SHA-256 b4578f35940bf5a1a655214a1cce5cab13eba73c1297cd78e1a04c2380b0152f

2. Face embedding: SFace (MobileFaceNet trained with the SFace loss), OpenCV Zoo
   face_recognition_sface_2021dec.onnx, by Yaoyao Zhong; ONNX conversion by Chengrui Wang.
   Source: https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/tree/main/models/face_recognition_sface
   Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0.
   Modified by Starbright Lab: the ONNX graph was rebuilt layer by layer as an NHWC
   TensorFlow graph with the original weights and converted (TensorFlow 2.16.1, matching the runtime) to TensorFlow Lite with
   dynamic-range int8 weights (face_recognition_sface_2021dec_int8dr.tflite).
   SHA-256 5bf1e5b705d5c0f26314b2b5dededf60b5c324b45cdf536654b67498df9e6f0c
