SHA-256 `e07423b2b153e774f43066b9d87e4c956e363da687d9968b1eac3c38e12bf389`.

Starting a replacement previously deleted `leave_note.m4a` before microphone
preparation. Preparation/start failures, a short recording, failed stop or empty
output could therefore remove or overwrite the last saved memo.

Each recorder now owns a unique staging file beside the saved memo. Finalization
publishes it by same-directory rename only after the existing 500 ms minimum,
successful stop and release, and nonempty regular-file output. Failed attempts
preserve the saved memo and clean only their own staging file. There is no
destination deletion or partial-copy fallback. Cleanup remains best-effort when
the filesystem denies deletion; abandoned staging after process death is not
reclaimed by this change.

The existing shared worker, cancellation, finalization on hide/release, playback
and committed deletion ordering remain. Stop's Boolean still reports the recording
outcome. Active Notes refreshes saved-file existence separately, retaining previous
playback/delete controls after failure with a truthful message. Repeated stops
coalesce while finalization is pending so an idle result cannot replace a failed
recording outcome. The inactive legacy Notes overlay is unchanged.
