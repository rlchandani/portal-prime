Screensaver — design mocks

Twelve premium screensaver modes, each auto-running its real animation with a mixed library of portraits, landscapes, squares & panoramas so you can see how each handles orientation on a wide screen. Click any to open it full-screen; press F11 for true fullscreen. Let it run ~30s to see transitions, Ken Burns, and (where relevant) cell swaps / collage beats.
Photos are placeholder samples (Picsum, fixed seeds) — the real build will use your library. All motion, layout, timing & transitions are the actual proposed behavior.
