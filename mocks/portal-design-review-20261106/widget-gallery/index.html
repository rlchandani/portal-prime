Eight widgets that belong in the room

A proposed Home widget family for the Family Area Portal. Sample data only; the product sends no commands from this page.

One family, three sizes

Sizes come straight from the existing 4×4 packed grid and its Small → Medium → Large cycle. The only addition is letting Photo take a full half of the screen (2×4, already legal under MAX_SPAN).

  • Small, 1×1 (434 × 235 dp)One fact. A number or a name you can read from the sofa.
  • Medium, 2×1 (892 × 235 dp)The fact plus its context: the next hours, the next event, the next three tasks.
  • Large, 2×2 (892 × 494 dp)A short list. Never a dashboard.
  • Photo, 2×4 or 2×2Full bleed, same corner radius, no title. It is the reason the object is in the room.
  • MaterialWarm card on warm ground, 36 dp radius, no border, no shadow, no blur. Photo and widgets share one edge language.
  • One accent, one meaningGreen appears only when something is live: a running timer, a light that is on, an event happening now.
  • Labels only when needed“16°” needs no “Weather” heading. Calendar says “Up next”; lists say how many are left.
  • Settings are not on HomeConfiguration moves to long-press and Customize. No widget carries a settings button.

The widgets

Each board shows Small, Medium and Large at true relative size. The lower row is what the widget looks like when it can't do its job, because that's the state a family sees on a bad day. A dashed outline marks a part that needs data the product doesn't have yet.
