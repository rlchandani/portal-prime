Anatomy
  • Shape: squircle, corner radius ≈ 26% of size (96px grid tile → 25px)
  • Fill: two-stop diagonal gradient (135°) in the app's accent — light stop top-left, saturated stop bottom-right
  • Gloss: top-down white veil 35% → 6% → transparent by 60% height — the "3D candy" finish
  • Shadow: 0 12px 24px −8px rgba(0,0,0,.65) — lifts the tile off the dark wallpaper
  • Glyph: white line icon, 2px stroke, 50% of tile size, optically centered
  • Label: Inter 13.5/500 at 92% white with a soft dark text-shadow
