App icons
One consistent squircle system: 24% continuous-corner radius, a soft drop shadow for depth, and a top-down gloss sheen — applied uniformly so every icon (stock app or ours) reads as a premium iOS tile.
Our custom icons (gradient tiles)
Stock apps (masked into the same squircle)
Anatomy
  • Shape: continuous-corner squircle, radius ≈ 24% of the icon size (110px → ~26px)
  • Shadow: 0 6px 16px −6px rgba(0,0,0,.35) for a lifted, tactile feel
  • Gloss: a subtle top-down white gradient (28%→0) for the "candy" finish
  • Custom icons: a diagonal 2–3 stop gradient in the app's accent + a centered white glyph
  • Stock icons: the app's own icon, center-cropped and masked to the squircle so it matches
  • Label: Inter 15/500, white, one line, 1px shadow for legibility on any wallpaper
