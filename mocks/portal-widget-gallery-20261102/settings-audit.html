Corrections proposed for the native iteration

Separate Customize Home from the web dashboard connection. Give Quick controls one truthful setup destination. Let Photo Memory edit its source while idle playback is off. Give Today links to all its data owners, and resolve the separate timer data stores.

The approved two-pane shell and Back/Forward history stay in place. These corrections remain proposals until design acceptance and native verification.
